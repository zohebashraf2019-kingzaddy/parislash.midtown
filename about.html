Since 2021

Since opening in 2021, Paris Lash Midtown has been dedicated to providing beautiful, high-quality services in a welcoming and comfortable space. We believe every guest deserves not only exceptional results, but an experience that makes them feel cared for from the moment they walk through our doors.

Today, Paris Lash is home to a talented team of independent lash artists who are passionate about their craft and committed to outstanding customer service. Whether you're visiting for your first set of lashes or you're one of our regulars, our goal is to help you leave feeling confident and refreshed.

In addition to our lash artists, Paris Lash Midtown offers private beauty suites for other beauty professionals: a modern, supportive space where independent artists grow their businesses while serving clients in a beautiful, central location.

We're proud to be a place where beauty professionals thrive and clients feel right at home.

Behind every great local business is a story

Meet Annie

Annie has been a lash artist for nearly eight years and has proudly owned Paris Lash for the past three. After purchasing the salon, she set out to make it her own while creating a space where clients feel comfortable, welcomed and excited to come back.

Over the past year, she's poured countless hours into renovating the salon into a warm, modern space in the heart of Reno. From the little details to the overall feel, her goal has always been an environment that's both beautiful and inviting.

Annie truly loves what she does, and it shows in every appointment. She's passionate about helping clients feel confident with customized lash sets that fit their style.
