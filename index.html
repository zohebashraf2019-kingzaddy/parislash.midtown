Meet TheOwner
Lead with passion, built with purpose

Hi, I'm Annie.

I've been a lash artist for nearly eight years and have proudly owned Paris Lash for the past three. After purchasing the salon, I set out to make it my own: a space where clients feel comfortable, welcomed and excited to come back.

This past year I poured countless hours into renovating the salon into a warm, modern space in the heart of Reno. I truly love what I do: creating customized lash sets that fit your style.
